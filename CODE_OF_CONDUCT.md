# Code of conduct

DontreeCrab is a small, friendly project. Please:

- be respectful and assume good intent,
- criticise ideas and code, not people,
- keep discussions on topic,
- do not post copyrighted karaoke files, lyrics, SoundFonts, or personal data,
- respect the licenses of third-party assets and dependencies.

Harassment, discrimination, doxxing and deliberate disruption are not welcome.
Maintainers may edit, lock or remove contributions that violate these rules.
